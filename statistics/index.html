Stats


This table summarises the high-level details for each day of my hike. If you click on the purple number in the first (“Day”) column, you will be taken to a detailed record of that day’s walk with a narrative description, photos, maps and a downloadable GPX file.

Note – this table does not display well on smartphones with a small screen

You can also access the daily logs via the interactive map – click here

The column headings are as follows:

Code Explanation
Day                 Number of days from start at Lizard
Date               Self explanatory (day, date, month, year)
h, m                Daily hours and minutes of walking time, including stops
Start point Where the walk started that morning
End point    Where the walk finished that evening
Mi                   Miles walked that day (but excluding walks for food in evening)
Cu mi            Cumulative miles walked from start at Lizard
Mi rem         Miles left to walk to the end at Dunnet
Ft                    Total feet ascended that day
Cu ft              Cumulative feet ascended from start at Lizard
Ft rem          Feet left to ascend to the end at Lizard
Acc                 Accommodation type at the end of the day’s walk

Acc key:
C Campsite (54 nights)
B Bed and breakfast (11 nights)
Y Youth Hostel  (1 night)
F Family and friends  (3 nights)
W Wild camp  (4 nights)
H Hotel  (6 nights)
